## Summary of changes for counsel

1. **Annual update**: CCPA (1798.130(a)(5)) expects the policy refreshed every 12
   months; the live policy will be ~19 months old at launch. New date added.
2. **Truthful cookie section**: the live policy says we "show you personalized
   ads/content" and count ad impressions. The rebuilt site does none of that.
   Rewritten to describe what actually exists: necessary device storage,
   opt-in Google Analytics 4, cookieless Vercel Analytics, and Dutchie's
   checkout cookies on their pages. Over-disclosure invites claims; this now
   matches reality.
3. **CPRA-era rights added**: right to correct, right to opt out of
   sale/sharing (with our statement that we do not sell or share), right to
   limit sensitive personal information (we do not collect it on this site),
   non-discrimination. Live policy predates these.

   loads for GPC browsers). Disclosed per CPPA guidance.
5. **Retention periods**: CPRA requires disclosure per category; added.
6. **Notice of Financial Incentive** for the PWF Rewards loyalty program
   (1798.125): material terms, how to join and leave, value statement. The live

# Privacy Policy (proposed text)

JB IP, LLC ("JB", "company", "us", "our", or "we") respects and is committed to
protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we
collect on jungleboys.com, how we use it, and the choices and rights you have.
The term Service Providers refers to entities meeting the definition in the
California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended ("CCPA"). Service Providers may use
Personal Information only for the purposes for which they are engaged.

## What we collect and why

**Browsing information.** Like every website, our servers receive your IP
address and basic technical information about your device. We use this in
aggregate to understand traffic, to protect the site, and to cooperate with
law enforcement where required.

**Analytics (optional).** With your consent, we use Google Analytics 4 to
understand how the site is used. Analytics does not run unless you choose
"Accept All" on our cookie notice, and never runs for browsers sending the
Global Privacy Control signal. We also use Vercel Analytics, which is
cookieless and does not identify you. We do not use advertising pixels,
session recording, or cross-site tracking on this site.

**Orders.** When you place an order, checkout is completed on pages operated
by our ordering partner Dutchie. Dutchie collects the information needed to
process your order (name, contact details, date of birth or ID verification,
and payment where applicable) under its own privacy policy. We do not receive
or store your payment card details or government ID on this website.

**Forms you submit.** If you contact us, apply to work with us, join our
wholesale or pheno programs, or sign up for updates, we store what you submit
(such as name, email, phone, and your message) together with a record of the
consent you gave, and we use it to respond and, where you agreed, to send you
marketing. These submissions are shared only with the Service Providers that
deliver these functions for us (for example our email and messaging
providers).

**PWF Rewards.** Our loyalty program is described in the Notice of Financial
Incentive below.

## Cookies and device storage

We use a small number of cookies and similar technologies:

- **Necessary storage** keeps the site working: your age-verification answer,
  your chosen store, your bag, your theme and accessibility preferences, and
  your cookie choice itself. These contain no personal details and are stored
  only on your device.
- **Analytics cookies** (Google Analytics) are set only after you choose
  "Accept All" on the cookie notice. You can decline them with "Necessary
  Only" and the site works identically.
- **Checkout cookies** are set by Dutchie on its checkout pages under its own
  policy.

You can clear or block cookies in your browser at any time. We honor the
Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal: if your browser sends it, analytics does
not load regardless of your cookie choice.

## We do not sell or share your Personal Information

We do not sell Personal Information, and we do not share it for cross-context
behavioral advertising. We have not done either in the preceding 12 months. We
do not knowingly collect Personal Information from anyone under 21; this site
is age-gated.

## Disclosure to third parties

We disclose Personal Information only: to Service Providers for the purposes
above; when required by law or legal process; to protect and defend the
rights or property of our company or visitors; to identify persons violating
the law or our Terms of Service; or in connection with a sale of all or part
of our company. We take reasonable precautions to keep Personal Information
secure, and we are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party
sites, including Dutchie's, which has its own policy.

may request deletion sooner at any time. Consent-gated analytics data is
retained per Google Analytics settings [COUNSEL: current GA4 retention is set

California residents may, at no charge and without discrimination, request:

- **To know** the categories and specific pieces of Personal Information we
  have collected, the sources, the purposes, and the third parties with whom

- **To delete** Personal Information we collected from you;
- **To correct** inaccurate Personal Information;
- **To opt out of sale or sharing** (not applicable, as we do neither); and
- **To limit use of sensitive Personal Information** (not applicable; we do
  not collect sensitive Personal Information on this website).

Make a request with the subject "california resident personal info request"
